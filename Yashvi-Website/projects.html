Stardust: High Power Rocket

Designed, built, and successfully launched a high-powered rocket earning my Level 1 High-Power Rocketry (L1 HPR) certification. The vehicle was powered by a Cesaroni 164H90-12A solid rocket motor and achieved an approximately 400 m apogee with a maximum velocity of 97.4 m/s. The project involved airframe design, propulsion and recovery integration, aerodynamic stability analysis, and flight-performance simulation. The successful flight validated the vehicle’s structural integrity, stability, and overall flight performance.
