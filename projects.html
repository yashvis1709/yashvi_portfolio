TMAD RC3 COMPETITION
Jan 2026 – Aug 2026

Remote Control Aircraft

Collaborated with a multidisciplinary team to design and build a fixed-wing RC aircraft capable of transporting a payload. I was primarily responsible for manufacturing and assembly, turning the CAD design into a physical airframe from individual components. I also worked on electrical wiring and routing, soldering, and connecting the onboard electrical systems. Throughout the build, I helped with testing and troubleshooting as we prepared the aircraft for flight.

Working under tight time constraints required rebuilding and modifying components as issues came up, while balancing mechanical, electrical, and manufacturing requirements. The project gave me hands-on experience taking an aircraft from design through manufacturing, integration, and flight testing.

MARS — LEAD
August 2026

Stardust: High-Powered Rocket

Designed, built, and successfully launched a high-powered rocket, earning my Level 1 High-Power Rocketry (L1 HPR) certification. The vehicle was powered by a Cesaroni 164H90-12A solid rocket motor and reached an approximately 400 m apogee with a maximum velocity of 97.4 m/s.

The project involved airframe design, propulsion and recovery integration, aerodynamic stability analysis, structural design, and flight-performance simulation. The successful flight provided a practical test of the vehicle's structural integrity, aerodynamic stability, recovery system, and overall flight performance.
